# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TriFusion Dynamics aims to maintain a welcoming, respectful and constructive environment for everyone who participates in project discussions, issues, reviews and contributions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, professional and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions; do not attack people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Give actionable feedback and accept constructive criticism.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct in project spaces, deliberate disruption and abusive language are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary. Reports can be sent privately through the [contact page](https://trifusiondynamics.vercel.app/contact). Please include links and enough context to investigate.

This code applies to project spaces operated by the maintainers. It is intended to support a safe, collaborative engineering community.
