# Sponsorship and Partnerships

Thank you for considering support for TriFusion Dynamics and the AgencyOS project.

## What support helps fund

Depending on the agreed scope, sponsorship can help with:

- Engineering time for reliability, security and maintainability.
- Documentation, onboarding and contributor experience.
- Test infrastructure, CI capacity and development tooling.
- Infrastructure and service costs for public demos.
- Research and prototypes for new integrations and modules.

## Ways to support

### Individual or community support

If a GitHub Sponsors profile is enabled for the maintainer, use the **Sponsor** button on the GitHub profile/repository. If no Sponsor button is visible, GitHub sponsorship is not currently configured; do not send payments to unofficial accounts.

### Company sponsorship

Companies interested in supporting a specific roadmap area, infrastructure cost or engineering initiative can start a conversation through the [TriFusion Dynamics contact page](https://trifusiondynamics.vercel.app/contact).

### Commercial partnership

For implementation work, integrations, custom modules, audits or managed engineering, use the [services page](https://trifusiondynamics.vercel.app/services) and contact form.

## Sponsorship transparency

Before accepting a company sponsorship, both parties should agree in writing on:

- Sponsorship amount, payment schedule and duration.
- Whether the support is unrestricted or earmarked for a named initiative.
- Recognition, logo placement and reporting expectations.
- Whether deliverables, support, response times or service levels are included.
- Intellectual-property, confidentiality and data-processing terms.

Sponsorship alone does not grant ownership, exclusivity, roadmap control, guaranteed features, support SLAs or preferential access. Any paid implementation or support commitment requires a separate written agreement.

## Current status

No public sponsorship tiers, payment URL, funding target or sponsor benefits are being advertised by this document. Contact the team to discuss a transparent arrangement.
